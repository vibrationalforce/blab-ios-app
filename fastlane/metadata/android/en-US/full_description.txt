Echoel - Bio-Reactive Performance Instrument

Your heartbeat becomes tempo. Your breath shapes the space. Echoel turns your body's signals into sound, image and light in real time — the body as controller.

KEY FEATURES

Bio-Reactive Audio Engine
• Heart rate → tempo, intensity, vibrato
• HRV → brightness, spatial width, harmonic complexity
• Coherence → harmonicity and texture
• Breathing → envelope and pacing

Sound Design
• Bio-reactive synthesis (additive DDSP, modal, cellular, sampler)
• Design and save your own patches
• Effects: reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, harmonizer

Composition & Beats
• Genre + key generation you keep editing in a real piano roll
• Concert pitch (A4) set exactly to the cent; optional BPM lock
• 16-step, 8-track beat maker and sampler

Connect
• MIDI / MPE in and out
• OSC and ADM-OSC immersive object output
• DMX / Art-Net lighting integration

Privacy
• Your biometrics stay on your device. No ads, no tracking.

Echoel is in active development. Feedback welcome: echoel@tropicaldrones.com

This is not a medical device. Biofeedback features are for creative and self-observation purposes only — not for diagnosis or treatment.

Permissions:
• Microphone: voice input and audio processing
• Bluetooth: MIDI controllers and heart-rate sensors
• Health Connect: heart rate and HRV data
