Brainstorming

What you actually get in TestFlight today — and where Echoel could go next. Ideas, not promises.

How to read this page. The first section is the honest current state: what a TestFlight tester can expect right now. The second is a brainstorming of future directions chosen for real market potential in the Apple ecosystem — these are exploration, not commitments. We keep this page current rather than over-promise.

⚠ This changelog stopped being updated after build 1889 (2026-06-17) — the current TestFlight build is far newer, and several items below were later REMOVED on purpose (AUv3, the drum sequencer, the piano roll, the Tools menu). Read it as history, not as today's state; Architecture carries the current status.

Build 1889 — iOS 18+, iPhone (2026-06-17). New at the time: live MIDI/MPE OUT (a virtual “Echoelmusic” source any DAW can record — standard or MPE), three new character controls (Virtuosity, Syncopation, Humanize) that reshape the generated lead, and a pro sampler engine (per-pad start/end trim, reverse, pitch) as the base for sample-based instruments. Plus click-free playing — reusing or stealing a still-ringing voice now glides into the new note instead of clicking. And build 1881's controls that play + a truer visual. Every value shows its type (Hz, s, BPM, semitones) and is exact to four decimals; press a value for a transparent vertical fader (up/down = fast sweep, sideways = fine, tap = type). New Transpose (±24 semitones; sub-bass & color follow) and Visual controls (Intensity/Detail/Motion/Spread, flash-capped <3 Hz). The immersive color is now derived from the heard tone — the pitch’s frequency transposed up whole octaves into the visible-light range (a direct frequency-to-wavelength mapping). Plus pinch-to-zoom for every eye and a realtime fix so the music re-seeds once per window, not in bursts. On build 1869's self-healing audio engine — the instrument auto-recovers from AirPods/Bluetooth connect & disconnect, sample-rate switches and system audio-graph rebuilds that used to leave it silent until relaunch (de-bounced, capped restart; clear “tap to retry” if recovery is exhausted; render path untouched). On build 1867's three new dimensions on the one instrument — a dedicated sub-bass / LFE voice (the “felt” Vibration dimension) that doubles the bass an octave down with a pushable level for sub/headphones/haptics; a real Metal GPU bio-visual (heart rate → pulse clamped to ≤2 Hz for flash safety, coherence → hue, breath → spread) reachable from the Tools menu and honouring Reduce Motion; and a fix so sound starts immediately on first open (it no longer waits for a camera pulse lock before playing). On top of build 1857's solid biofeedback (bus freshness window + BLE auto-reconnect) and build 1855's deep composition pass (real chord motion, expressive phrasing, seamless bar-boundary morph, crackle-free 8-voice synthesis).

In TestFlight now Live

The body as controller — sound, composition and pro I/O, on-device.

Bio input

iPhone camera (rPPG), Apple Watch & HealthKit, and universal Bluetooth heart-rate straps (0x180D). HR / HRV / coherence computed on device. A clearly-labeled Demo source means it always plays without hardware.

Bio-reactive synthesis

Additive DDSP plus a polyphonic pad/lead and a dedicated sub-bass — silent until you arm it. Heart rate, HRV, breath and coherence drive tempo, brightness, harmonicity and texture.

Composition

Pick a genre and key; your body generates the material and you shape it live — there is no note editor, on purpose. Eight curated genres (Self-Observation, Deep Ambient, Drift, Contemplation, Vaporwave, Sci-Fi, Classical, Dub Techno) and 50 scales. Concert pitch (A4) set exactly to the cent; optional BPM lock for tight loops.

Character controls

Liveliness, Darkness, Tension, Romance and Weird shape the composition alongside the bio signal — every parameter takes exact numeric entry. Liveliness, Tension and Weird are continuous; Darkness and Romance are thresholds, and the panel says so.

Effects

Reverb (continuous room size), delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation and a harmonizer — plus stampable production characters (Underwater, Telephone, Cassette, Vinyl, Dream, Blurry…).

Keep what you played

Retroactive loop capture from an always-on buffer; export audio (WAV) for your DAW.

Pro connectivity

MIDI input (notes, pitch bend, CC 74), OSC output, ADM-OSC immersive object output, and native Art-Net and sACN light output for installations and stage.

Siri & Shortcuts

Start a session, stop, or keep the last loop by voice or automation.

Private & accessible

Everything on-device — no account, no ads, no tracking. Designed accessibility-first (VoiceOver, large legible readouts, 3 Hz flash cap). For music and self-observation, not medical diagnosis.

Deliberately not in Echoelmusic: drums and a step sequencer, a note editor (piano roll), a sampler, an arrangement timeline, multitrack recording, video editing, live RTMP streaming, and AUv3 — as a plugin or as a host. Most of these were built and then removed in July 2026: this is an instrument, and each of them was a second product to maintain. Not yet shipping: the standalone Apple Watch app, App Clip, and the on-device AI / audio-analysis ideas listed below.

Brainstorming — future directions Idea

Apple-ecosystem expansions chosen for real market potential. Validated as directions, not committed as features.

Plays to Apple's strengths now

AUv3 as the wedge — tried and dropped

The idea: a fully MPE-expressive bio-modulation plugin living inside Logic, AUM, Cubasis and Ableton, on the reasoning that producers already pay for plugins. Outcome: it was built, shipped, and then deliberately removed in July 2026 — a plugin is a second product with its own host-compatibility matrix, and Echoel is a standalone instrument you play with your body. Not on the roadmap. Recorded here because the reasoning was sound and the decision was not.

Standalone Apple Watch capture

On-wrist HR/HRV streaming with an Extended Runtime session, so you can perform with the watch and leave the phone in your pocket. Why: the watch is the natural bio sensor; unlocks fitness-music crossover and hands-free performance.

Foundation Models — on-device “band director”

An on-device model that turns your live bio-state into arrangement choices (genre, key, mood, effects). Why: generative music is a hot category; on-device + biometric is a genuinely differentiated angle — and it never leaves the device, which is our promise.

Music Understanding — jam with your library

Analyse an imported track's key, tempo and structure, then generate bio-reactive material in key with it. Why: “play along with any song using your heartbeat” is broadly appealing and very shareable.

Widgets & Live Activities

Glanceable coherence/HR, and a Live Activity during a session. Why: low-cost retention and daily re-engagement.

Focus filters & automations

Tie sessions to Focus modes and Shortcuts automations. Why: meets users where their routines already are; deepens the Siri integration that's already live.

Bigger bets — premium & immersive

visionOS immersive bio-space

A spatial, bio-reactive audio-visual environment on Vision Pro (the ADM-OSC immersive object output is already in place). Why: premium immersive art has few credible competitors and a high price ceiling.

Apple TV / large-screen installation mode

Bio-reactive visuals for galleries, venues, retail and events. Why: installations and events are a real B2B revenue line, not just consumer downloads.

HomeKit / Matter lighting

Drive smart home lights from your bio signal (beyond pro Art-Net/DMX). Why: consumer smart-home crossover — “your home breathes with you” — broadens reach beyond musicians.

AirPods head-tracking as a controller

Use head motion and AirPods spatial data as an expressive input. Why: a novel, hands-free control surface unique to Apple hardware.

SharePlay group bio-jam

A shared session over FaceTime where each participant's body adds a layer (each device on-device). Why: social, viral, and demos beautifully.

Reach, discovery & monetization

App Clip at installations

Scan a code at an installation or show to instantly play the bio-instrument — no install. Why: turns physical presence into a viral discovery funnel.

One-time Pro unlock (StoreKit 2)

Keep the core free; unlock pro export, immersive output and advanced AI once, no subscription. Why: revenue that respects the “no lock-in” brand and converts pros.

Accessibility-first music creation

Make music with your heartbeat and Switch Control / eye-tracking when hands aren't an option. Why: a genuinely underserved audience, strong PR and grant potential, and on-brand.

Education (STEAM)

Biofeedback + music as a classroom tool. Why: a durable institutional market with its own funding.

Deeper DAW handoff

Stem export, richer MIDI, Ableton Link. Why: the more it fits real producer workflows, the stickier it gets.

Platforms & compatibility — “Everything wires”
The principle. Echoel doesn't have to look or work identically on every device. It speaks open standards — MIDI 2.0/MPE, OSC, ADM-OSC, Bluetooth Heart Rate, Art-Net/DMX — so any hardware can wire into the same bio-reactive core, each in the form that fits it. The iPhone build in TestFlight is the stable anchor; everything below is an open construction site — documented directions, not commitments.

Apple — one core, many surfaces Anchor

iPhone today; iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro as adaptive surfaces of the same engine. Why: the anchor platform — ship stable here first, expand by surface.

Android client

A native client speaking the same open standards (Health Connect for bio, BLE straps, OSC/MIDI out). Why: the largest device base; reachable precisely because we avoid SDK lock-in.

Windows & Linux desktop

A standalone build for producers and installations; Linux for embedded and gallery rigs. (Standalone only — the plugin route was tried and dropped, see above.) Why: desktop is where DAW work and permanent installations live.

Adaptive WebApp

A zero-install browser entry using Web Bluetooth, Web MIDI and Web Audio for instant try-and-create on any OS. Why: the lowest-friction funnel — share a link, the body makes music.

XR / VR

Immersive bio-space — visionOS first, OpenXR for broader headsets. Why: the most expressive home for bio-reactive audio-visual art.

AI computers & edge devices

The bio→music core as an embeddable engine for new on-device-AI hardware and smart rooms. Why: “everything wires” — the instrument is the standard, the hardware is interchangeable.
