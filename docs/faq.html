FAQ

Everything you need to know about Echoelmusic

General

What is Echoelmusic?
Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive generative music instrument that transforms your biometric signals (heart rate, HRV, breathing) into harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in real time, which you export as .wav or as a MIDI file (.mid) for your DAW. GPU bio-reactive visuals and Art-Net/sACN lighting output ship today; video editing, multitrack recording and live broadcast are deliberately not part of it. Coming to iPhone first, with iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro planned.
Which features are available at launch?
At launch Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive music instrument: bio-reactive synthesis (EchoelSynth), generative composition of harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in any key and BPM (EchoelGen), GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals in a movable window or fullscreen (EchoelVis, flash-safe), Art-Net and sACN lighting output (EchoelLux), export to .wav or .mid for Ableton, Logic and FL Studio, MIDI 2.0-ready input from an external controller (EchoelMIDI), biometrics (EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath via HealthKit, Bluetooth straps like Polar H10, and camera rPPG with a live control display), OSC and ADM-OSC sync (EchoelNet), and coherence / self-observation. DMX-512 interfaces, projection mapping, on-device AI, spatial audio and Ableton Link are on the roadmap, not in the app today. Video editing, multitrack recording and live streaming are not planned — they were built and removed in July 2026. The Architecture page lists exact current status.
What makes it "bio-reactive"?
Bio-reactive means the software responds to your body in real time. EchoelBio captures your biometrics — HRV, heart rate, breath, and coherence — and feeds them to the sound engine in real time. Your heart rate influences tempo, filter brightness and vibrato; your coherence level is the strongest single driver — brightness, filter cutoff, harmonic content, level and noise; HRV shifts brightness and overtone spread; and your breathing shapes the amplitude envelope. Your body becomes the controller.
Do I need special hardware?
No — just your iPhone. The camera pulse works with no extra hardware: cover the rear camera + flash with a fingertip and Echoel reads your heartbeat (photoplethysmography) in real time. For the lowest latency and HRV ground-truth, pair a standard Bluetooth heart-rate device (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin/CooSpo straps, or a watch in HR-broadcast mode). An Apple Watch works via Apple Health (slightly higher latency). For MIDI, any class-compliant controller connects instantly.
Does it work with my Oura Ring or smartwatch?
Yes, via Apple Health — enable your device's Apple Health sync (Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, etc.) and Echoel reads heart rate and HRV from there. One honest caveat about latency: the Oura Ring (incl. Ring 4) exposes no real-time Bluetooth to third-party apps — it pairs only with the Oura app, and its data reaches Echoel through Apple Health with a delay (not beat-to-beat). For a latency-free live experience, use the camera pulse or a standard Bluetooth heart-rate device — which is exactly what Oura itself recommends for live heart rate.
What are the EchoelTools?
Echoelmusic's engines are organized into unified tools. Some ship today; the rest are the wider vision, in active development:
  • EchoelSynth — Bio-reactive synthesis: a DDSP harmonic-plus-noise voice, a polyphonic pad/lead and a dedicated sub-bass
  • EchoelGen — Generative composition: harmonic, melodic & rhythmic loops in any key and BPM
  • EchoelFX — Filter, saturation, tape, bitcrush, harmonizer, chorus/flanger/phaser/tremolo, delay, reverb, widener, compressor, limiter (modelled analog compressors Planned)
  • EchoelMIDI — MIDI 2.0-ready input (notes, pitch bend, CC 74); virtual MIDI 1.0 out behind a routing switch. MPE zones, touch instruments and Audio→MIDI Planned
  • EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath & LF/HF analysis (HealthKit, Bluetooth straps, camera rPPG)
  • EchoelNet — OSC & ADM-OSC immersive object output
  • EchoelVis — GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals: four generative looks, flash-safe, floating window or fullscreen
  • EchoelLux — Art-Net + sACN lighting output over open standards (DMX-512 interfaces & smart home Planned)
  • EchoelMix Cut — a console mixer and multi-track recorder would make this a workstation; LUFS-aware mastering on export stays
  • EchoelVid Cut — video editing was built and removed, RTMP was never built; the immersive visual still records to MP4
  • EchoelStage Live — External-display output ships today (USB-C/HDMI or AirPlay); projection mapping Planned
  • EchoelAI Planned — On-device CoreML intelligence & AI stem separation
Every tool talks to every other tool in real time.

Instruments & Synthesis

What synthesis engines are included?
All synthesis is unified inside EchoelSynth — one tool with three voices:
  • DDSP — Differentiable DSP (harmonic + noise model), the bio-reactive core
  • Poly — a polyphonic pad/lead with filter, envelope, unison and vibrato
  • Sub — a dedicated sub-bass voice you feel as much as hear
Your biometrics compose harmonic and melodic material in any key and BPM — pads, chords, leads and a sub-bass voice — mapped directly to sound parameters in real time. There is no drum engine and no sampler: percussion was removed in July 2026 so the instrument stays one thing. Your own voice can become the timbre: hold a tone in the Sound panel’s Voice timbre row and Echoel measures its spectral envelope — about 64 numbers, no audio recorded and none stored; a saved patch can carry that envelope under a name you give it. Render to .wav, or export a MIDI file (.mid), for your DAW.
What touch instruments are available?
The primary instrument is your body — and any external MIDI controller you plug in plays the built-in performer voice — one monophonic voice, driven by notes and pitch bend. In-app you shape the sound in the patch editor and cut what your body played into loops. There is deliberately no note editor and no drum grid: Echoelmusic is about the sound being made now, not about drawing notes after the fact. Dedicated touch instruments (chord pad, melody XY pad, multi-octave keyboard, strum pad) are on the roadmap, not in the app today.
Are there orchestral instruments?
Echoelmusic is a synthesizer, not a sample library. EchoelSynth offers naturally-voiced DDSP timbre presets — violin, cello, clarinet, oboe, flute, trumpet and more. These are living synthesis models shaped by your body in real time, not multisampled orchestral sections. Full orchestral libraries with articulations are not a goal.

Effects & DSP

How many effects are there?
All effects are unified inside EchoelFX — one bio-reactive chain, built natively with zero external dependencies. Shipping today: reverb, tape-style delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, bitcrush, stereo widener and filter, plus LoFi characters (Underwater, Telephone, Cassette, Vinyl, Dream, Megaphone) and a mastering stage (EQ → compressor → limiter with true-peak trim). Body signals can modulate FX parameters live. Every effect is a real DSP algorithm — nothing is a placeholder.
What analog-inspired processing is included?
The saturation stage adds analog-style drive and warmth, the tape-style delay brings wow-and-flutter echo character, and the LoFi characters (Cassette, Vinyl, Telephone and more) give a take instant vintage texture. A dedicated suite of named analog emulations is on the roadmap, not in the app today — we only list what ships.

Composing & Music Creation

Does Echoelmusic compose music for me?
No — and that's by design. Echoelmusic is a creativity tool, not an AI that generates finished pieces. Your biometric data guides the musical parameters — tempo from your heart rate, harmonic variation from your HRV, dynamic expression from your breathing — but you are always the creator. We evolve your creativity, we don't replace it.
What composing tools are available?
EchoelGen composes for you from your body:
  • Generative loops — Harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops composed in real time, in any key and BPM
  • Bio-reactive variation — The composition mutates with your bio-state: calm states produce ordered, settled phrases; higher arousal adds variation
  • Coherence & self-observation — Lock to a BPM and create, or simply breathe and watch the music respond
  • Export — Render any take to .wav — or export it as a MIDI file (.mid) — and drop it into Ableton, Logic or FL Studio

MIDI & Hardware

What MIDI features are supported?
All MIDI lives inside EchoelMIDI. MIDI 2.0-ready input (Universal MIDI Packets — parses both 1.0 and 2.0 traffic): notes and pitch bend reach the built-in performer voice — one monophonic voice. Slide (CC 74) opens that voice’s filter and Press (channel pressure) swells it — both channel-wide, both reaching the one voice. Air-CC (CC 21–31) is parsed and carried on the control bus, but no voice consumes it. Output is a virtual “Echoelmusic” MIDI 1.0 source any DAW can record, created once you enable a MIDI route in the routing panel. MPE output ships: turn on the two switches in the routing panel and notes go out over member channels 2–16, each carrying Glide (pitch bend), Slide (CC 74) and Press (channel pressure), with the zone announced. MPE input zone handling (per-note channels, pressure), native MIDI 2.0 out, inter-app tempo sync and audio-to-MIDI are on the roadmap, not in the app today.
What about recording and export?
Today Echoelmusic exports your take two ways: WAV (lossless) — auto-named with artist, date, key and BPM — and Standard MIDI File (.mid), which carries the melody with tempo, 4/4 and the key signature, anchored to whole bars so it lands on the grid in Ableton, Logic or FL Studio and loops seamlessly. The MIDI file is melody only; there is no drum track, because there are no drums in the instrument. Importing a .mid is not in this build. Multi-track recording, FLAC/ALAC and video editing are not planned — Echoelmusic is an instrument, and mixing belongs in your DAW.
Does Echoelmusic work as a plugin or a host?
Neither — it is a standalone instrument. Echoelmusic is not an AUv3 Audio Unit plugin, and it does not host third-party AUv3 plugins. Both existed in earlier builds and were deliberately removed: an instrument you play with your body is not a plugin surface, and maintaining one is a second product. It reaches your DAW the open way instead — WAV export, plus live OSC / ADM-OSC and Art-Net / sACN output for stage rigs. (A virtual MIDI source exists but only appears once you enable a MIDI route in the routing panel. MIDI file export of the generated take ships today — it is in the export panel next to WAV. MPE output ships too, behind two switches in the routing panel.) VST3 and CLAP are not planned.

Visuals & Lighting

What visual modes are available?
GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals ship today. EchoelVis renders four generative looks — Rings, Water, Aurora and Depth — driven live by your heart rate, coherence and breath, in a movable floating window or fullscreen for projection. Flash rate is capped under 3 Hz (WCAG epilepsy guidance) and Reduce Motion is honoured. Richer generative and AR worlds remain the wider vision, in active development.
Can I control lights and lasers?
Yes — Art-Net and sACN (E1.31) lighting output ship today. Your heartbeat can drive professional fixtures (moving heads, LED bars) natively over open network standards — no vendor SDK, with flash safety built in. Echoelmusic also speaks OSC and ADM-OSC live, so your body can drive a TouchDesigner or Resolume rig in real time. Also live: EchoelStage external-display output (USB-C/HDMI/AirPlay — the visual takes the connected screen). On the roadmap: DMX-512 interfaces, smart-home lights, projection mapping and a cue system. Today you can mirror the fullscreen visual to a projector via AirPlay.

Video & Streaming

What video features are included?
Video editing is not part of Echoelmusic. An NLE with a timeline, multi-cam, ProRes and color grading was built and then removed in July 2026 — it is a second product, not an instrument. What ships is audio and light: your heartbeat composes music you export as WAV to Ableton, FL Studio or any DAW, plus the immersive visual, which you can record as a share-ready MP4. You can also export the take as a MIDI file for your DAW.
Can I stream live?
Live streaming (RTMP) is not planned. It was scoped and cut — a broadcast stack is a second product. Today you create and export. For immersive and stage work, Echoelmusic already speaks open standards live — OSC, ADM-OSC (immersive audio objects), Art-Net and sACN (stage lighting) — so a performer's body can drive a TouchDesigner / Resolume / lighting rig in real time.

Intelligence

What AI features does Echoelmusic have?
On-device AI is on the roadmap, not in the app today. The vision for EchoelAI is entirely on-device via CoreML — AI stem separation to isolate vocals, bass and instruments, with no cloud and full privacy. In active development.

Collaboration & Immersive

Can I collaborate with others?
Yes — EchoelNet provides one-way OSC (Open Sound Control) output to DAWs, VJ software, and custom applications, plus a virtual MIDI 1.0 source any DAW can record live once a MIDI route is enabled — with MPE output over it once you turn on the two routing switches. Bidirectional OSC and Ableton Link tempo sync are on the roadmap.
What about immersive and spatial setups?
Echoel is iPhone-first today. For immersive and spatial work it acts as a bio-reactive object source: the body (breath, coherence, HRV) drives an audio object's position over ADM-OSC — the open Audio Definition Model over Open Sound Control standard — into object-based renderers such as Adamson FletcherMachine, L-ISA and d&b Soundscape. No vendor SDK; open standards only. A dedicated Vision Pro app, on-device immersive scenes and other Apple platforms are on the roadmap, not yet shipped — see the Architecture page for what is live today versus planned.

Health & Privacy

Is this a medical device?
No. Echoelmusic is a creative tool, not a medical device. Biofeedback features (heart rate, HRV, coherence scores) are for creative and self-observation purposes only. They are not clinically validated measurements. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice. See our Health Disclaimer.
Photosensitivity warnings?
Some visual effects include flashing lights, rapid color changes, and laser patterns. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or seizure conditions, consult a physician before use. Accessibility options can reduce or disable motion and flashing effects.
Is my data safe?
All biometric data is processed locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Your device's own on-device Data Protection encrypts local storage. See our Privacy Policy and Security page.

Accessibility

Is Echoelmusic accessible?
We build with accessibility in mind. Today the app provides VoiceOver labels on the primary controls, reduced-motion support, a dark high-contrast interface, and a safety cap that keeps any visual movement at or below 3 Hz (W3C WCAG epilepsy guidance). WCAG 2.2 AA is our target as the app grows; broader accessibility modes, color-vision options and additional languages are planned, not in the app today. The app currently ships in English. See our Accessibility page.
