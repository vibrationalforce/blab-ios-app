Complete Overview

Echoelmusic — physical computing and biofeedback for music. One signal chain — your body — composes sound in real time, ready to export to your DAW. GPU bio-reactive visuals and Art-Net/sACN light output ship today; video editing and live broadcast are deliberately not part of it.

App — Coming Soon

Your heartbeat and breath compose music in real time. Generate a loop or a whole piece from your body — to calm down and observe yourself, or to lock to a BPM and export to Ableton, Logic, or FL Studio for your track. Bio-reactive synthesis, generative composition, and open-standards output (MIDI, OSC, ADM-OSC, Art-Net, sACN) ship today. Video editing, multitrack recording, an arrangement timeline and live broadcast are deliberately not part of Echoelmusic — it is an instrument, not a workstation.

The 12 Engines

Every tool talks to every other tool through one creative workspace — biology in, art out. Full capability breakdown on the Tools Brainstorming and Architecture pages.

EchoelSynth

Bio-reactive synthesis — a DDSP harmonic-plus-noise voice, a polyphonic pad/lead and a dedicated sub-bass, driven by your heartbeat, breath and coherence.

EchoelFX

EQ, dynamics, reverb, delay, modulation, drive, mastering + analog emulations.

EchoelGen

Generative composition — harmonic, melodic & rhythmic loops in any key and BPM, exported to .wav.

EchoelMIDI

MIDI 2.0-ready input (UMP) — notes, pitch bend, CC 74; virtual MIDI 1.0 out behind a routing switch. Planned: MPE zones, MPE out, audio-to-MIDI.

EchoelBio

HRV, heart rate, breath, coherence — HealthKit, Bluetooth straps (Polar H10) & camera rPPG with a live control display.

EchoelNet

OSC (EchoelSync) and ADM-OSC immersive object output, live today.

EchoelMix Cut

A console and multi-track recorder would make this a workstation. LUFS-aware mastering on export stays; the rest belongs in your DAW.

EchoelVis

Metal GPU bio-visual live today — heartbeat drives the pulse, coherence the hue, breath the spread. Particles, Hilbert map, generative & AR worlds are Planned.

EchoelVid Cut

Video editing and RTMP streaming were built and removed in July 2026. What remains is the immersive visual you can record as a share-ready clip.

EchoelLux

Native Art-Net and sACN lighting live today, flash-safe by design. DMX-512, HomeKit smart lighting and a cue system are Planned.

EchoelStage Planned

External displays, projection mapping, multi-screen output.

EchoelAI Planned

On-device CoreML, stem separation. Private, fast, no cloud.

How it works

1 — Sense
Apple Watch, a Bluetooth heart-rate strap or the iPhone camera reads your heartbeat in real time; the camera also estimates your breathing. No setup.
2 — Transform
Biology composes the loop. Coherence opens the harmony; your pulse drives motion; breath shapes the envelope.
3 — Export
Render to .wav and drop it into your DAW. Visuals and light go out live over Art-Net, sACN, OSC and ADM-OSC.

Formats & Standards

Every input and output format the platform speaks — what's available at launch, and what's planned.

Audio engine
48 kHz, 32-bit float internal; buffer 128 / 256 / 512 frames (2.67 / 5.3 / 10.7 ms).
Audio export
At launch: WAV (lossless), normalized to the chosen loudness target; 30 s CAF pre-roll capture. Planned: AAC/M4A (the encoder exists, the export path forces WAV), FLAC / ALAC, stem export.
Spatial audio
At launch: stereo with HRV-reactive spatial reverb character, plus ADM-OSC immersive object out (breath→azimuth, coherence→distance, HRV→elevation) into object-based renderers (L-ISA, d&b Soundscape, Adamson FletcherMachine). Planned: Dolby Atmos authoring, head-tracked binaural for AirPods, multichannel (5.1 / 7.1.4) and ambisonics.
MIDI
At launch: MIDI 2.0-ready input (UMP — parses 1.0 and 2.0) carrying notes, channel pitch bend, slide/timbre CC74 and air-CC; Echoel publishes a virtual CoreMIDI source any DAW can record (MIDI 1.0 over UMP), created once a MIDI route is enabled in the routing panel. Also at launch: Standard MIDI File (.mid) export — the take's melody with tempo, 4/4 and key signature, anchored to whole bars so it lands on a DAW grid in the right key and loops seamlessly. Planned: full MPE zone handling (RPN 6,6) and channel pressure, MPE out, native MIDI 2.0 out, and .mid import.
Video
The immersive visual records to H.264 MP4 with the live audio muxed in — a share-ready clip. A video editor (ProRes, multi-cam, LUT grading) is not planned: it was built and removed in July 2026.
Visuals & mapping
At launch: Metal GPU bio-visual (heart rate to pulse, coherence to hue, breath to spread), flash-safe. Planned: richer 120 fps modes, chroma key, projection mapping (warp + edge-blend), multi-screen / external-display output, NDI & Syphon video-out, AirPlay.
Lighting
At launch: native Art-Net (UDP 6454) and sACN / E1.31 unicast, zero-dependency, with flash safety. Planned: DMX-512 over USB, HomeKit smart lighting, fixture profiles & cue lists.
Networking & sync
At launch: OSC 1.0 over UDP (EchoelSync) — 6 bio addresses, default localhost:8000. Planned: Ableton Link tempo/phase sync, bidirectional OSC.
Streaming
Not planned. RTMP / RTMPS was scoped and cut — a broadcast stack is a second product. Echoelmusic goes out live over OSC, ADM-OSC, Art-Net and sACN instead.

Privacy

All biometric data is processed locally on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs — your data never leaves your hardware. Any on-device AI on the roadmap is designed to keep that same promise. See Privacy and Security for details.

Health & Safety

Echoelmusic is a creative tool for self-observation, not a medical device and not for diagnosis.

  • Do not use brainwave-entrainment modes while operating vehicles or machinery.
  • Do not use under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Echoelmusic is not a therapeutic device; never change medication based on what you see in the app — coordinate any changes with your provider.
  • Visual flash rate is capped at 3 Hz (W3C WCAG epilepsy compliance).
