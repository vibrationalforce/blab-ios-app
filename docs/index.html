Coherence

The science, measured — not mystified

Biofeedback as a real signal you can see. Shown, never promised.

Measured

RMSSD, SDNN and pNN50 from your beat-to-beat intervals — the standard time-domain HRV metrics, computed the same way for every sensor.

Frequency-domain coherence

A real spectrum of your heartbeat (Lomb–Scargle / Welch) read in the standard HRV bands. Coherence is the share of power gathered in the ~0.1 Hz resonance peak — a number, not a vibe.

Resonance breathing

An optional guide paces slow breathing near 6 breaths/min (~0.1 Hz). Slow paced breathing is shown to raise vagally-mediated HRV (Lehrer & Gevirtz). The guide never forces a pace and never asks you to hold your breath.

Honest limits

A chest strap (e.g. Polar H10) is ground truth; wrist and camera readings are estimates. This is for self-observation — not medical diagnosis, and no “healing frequencies”.

Vibration

One body, many dimensions.

Sound, space, light, and vibration — played live by your physiology, over open standards.

Your body is the controller

Every heartbeat, breath, and move shapes sound and light in real time. No MIDI. No knobs. Just you.

Bio-generative music

Harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in any key and BPM, composed by your heartbeat and breath. Export to .wav and MIDI, or run as an AUv3 plugin inside Logic Pro, GarageBand or AUM.

Living visuals

A Metal GPU bio-renderer paints with your body in ten generative looks — heart rate to pulse, coherence to hue, breath to spread — in a movable window or fullscreen for projection. Flash rate held under 3 Hz for safety.

Open spine

Send your body anywhere it needs to go: OSC, MIDI 2.0 and MPE in & out, ADM-OSC immersive objects, AUv3. Video capture, live streaming, Ableton Link and on-device AI are on the roadmap.

Your pulse becomes light

Stream your body to stage and venue rigs over open lighting standards — native Art-Net and sACN, no vendor SDK. Your heartbeat drives the room, with flash safety built in.
