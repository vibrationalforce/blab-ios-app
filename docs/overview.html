Complete Overview

Echoelmusic — physical computing and biofeedback for art. One signal chain — your body — drives sound, visuals, video, and light for installation, event, cinema, theater, performance, and live broadcast.

How it works

1 — Sense
Apple Watch, Polar H10 or iPhone camera read heartbeat, breathing and movement in real time. No setup.
2 — Transform
Biology shapes sound, visuals and light. Coherence opens the mix; your pulse drives the bass; breath shapes the envelope.
3 — Radiate
Record it, film it, stream it, light up a room — from living room to festival stage.

Formats & Standards

Every input and output format the platform speaks — what's available at launch, and what's planned.

Audio engine
48 kHz, 32-bit float internal; buffer 128 / 256 / 512 frames (2.67 / 5.3 / 10.7 ms).
Audio export
At launch: WAV (lossless) and AAC/M4A, LUFS-normalized to −14 LUFS; 30 s CAF pre-roll capture. Planned: FLAC / ALAC, stem export.
Spatial audio
At launch: stereo with HRV-reactive spatial reverb character. Planned: 3D object panning, Dolby Atmos / spatial-audio rendering, head-tracked rendering for AirPods, multichannel (5.1 / 7.1.4) and ambisonics.
MIDI
At launch: MIDI 2.0 + MPE input (per-note pitch bend, slide/timbre CC74, air-CC). Planned: Standard MIDI File (.mid) import/export, MIDI output.
Video
At launch: H.264 MP4, 720×1280 (9:16), 30 fps, AAC audio, 15 / 30 / 60 s short-form. Planned: H.265/HEVC, Apple ProRes 422/4444, 1080p & 4K, 24/25/30/60 fps, landscape & square, LUT colour grading.
Visuals & mapping
At launch: Metal render at 120 fps, 5 visual modes, 6-pass chroma key. Planned: projection mapping (warp + edge-blend), multi-screen / external-display output, NDI & Syphon video-out, AirPlay.
Lighting
Planned: DMX-512 (USB / Ethernet), Art-Net 4 (UDP 6454), HomeKit smart lighting, fixture profiles & cue lists.
Networking & sync
At launch: OSC 1.0 over UDP (EchoelSync) — 6 bio addresses, default localhost:8000. Planned: Ableton Link tempo/phase sync, bidirectional OSC.
Streaming
Planned: RTMP / RTMPS to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook and custom endpoints (HaishinKit).

Privacy

All biometric data is processed locally on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. AI runs entirely on-device — your data never leaves your hardware. See Privacy and Security for details.

Health & Safety

Echoelmusic is a creative tool for self-observation, not a medical device and not for diagnosis.

  • Do not use brainwave-entrainment modes while operating vehicles or machinery.
  • Do not use under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • For therapeutic use, coordinate any medication changes with your provider.
  • Visual flash rate is capped at 3 Hz (W3C WCAG epilepsy compliance).
