**Source:** Owner-provided competitive analysis (Loopy Pro 2.0, Bitwig, Ableton, Logic, Reaper, TouchDesigner, Resolume, MadMapper, VDMX, Notch, Disguise, Maze Tools, EEG/HRV hardware).

The source document over-states current capabilities (claims 1,552 commits, 12 tools, EngineBus pre-existing, BioEventGraph implemented, EchoelVis 8 modes, iOS 15+). ## 1 · Adopted strategic position

**New positioning line** (replaces "Make Beats. Record Video. Stream Live." and the implicit "better than all DAWs + mapping + video combined"):

> **Echoelmusic — the first bio-reactive performance instrument.
> What Loopy Pro, Bitwig, and TouchDesigner together cannot do.**

Rationale: the only structural differentiator we credibly own is **biofeedback as first-class modulation source** across the EngineBus — every other axis (DAW maturity, plugin ecosystem, projection-mapping depth, GPU visuals, VJ tooling) is years behind market leaders and not catchable solo. The positioning concedes those axes deliberately so the remaining axis (bio-native architecture) reads as a deliberate choice, not an incomplete copy.

## 5 · Trigger-based strategy shifts

If any of these happen before EchoelLoop ships, the plan changes:

- **Loopy Pro ships OSC + sensor-input in App Store version** → differentiation collapses on workflow axis. Pivot to scientific-DSP depth (BioEventGraph, Polar H10 + Muse validation studies) as the moat.
- **Apple ships HealthKit mapping in Logic Pro / Logic Pad** → accelerate visionOS-first cycle; Apple won't compete there for 2+ years.
- **TouchDesigner ships an iOS companion** → reposition Echoel from "installation tool" to "performer tool" — keep BeatTab + EchoelLoop, sacrifice EchoelStage ambition.
- **Suno/Udio integrate biofeedback input** → unlikely (their model is text-to-audio), but reframe EchoelAI as "live bio-reactive co-performer" not "generative composer".
