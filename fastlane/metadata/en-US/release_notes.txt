Echoel is in active development. Feedback and collaborators welcome: echoel@tropicaldrones.com

New in this build

Composition
- Eighteen curated genres: Self-Observation, Deep Ambient, Drift, Contemplation, Vaporwave, Sci-Fi, Classical, Dub Techno, Acid Techno, Deep House, Deep Tech, Dark Minimal, Uplifting Trance, Tech House, Minimal Techno, Detroit Techno, Still Drone and Ambient Pulse
  (all genres are currently drum-free — Echoelmusic has no drum engine; the genres differ in harmony, timbre and motion)
- Eight character controls: Liveliness, Darkness, Tension, Romance, Weird, Virtuosity, Syncopation, Humanize
- Concert pitch (A4) set exactly to the cent; key selection and optional BPM lock

Voice
- Your voice becomes the instrument's timbre: hold a tone ("Voice timbre" → Capture) — measured as a spectral envelope, never recorded or stored
- Save the measured colour into a patch, always with a name label
- "Tune to key": optionally pulls your microphone monitoring into the session key — only what you hear in your headphones, never the music

Sound
- Algorithmic reverb with room and hall presets for real depth per genre
- Harmonizer effect: third and fifth harmony voices above the melody — optional "Follow the key": the third breathes diatonically with the key
- Per-genre saturation, from clean classical to heavily driven
- Anti-aliasing smoothing for cleaner high notes and vibrato

Workflow
- Retroactive capture of the loop you just played
- Precise controls and numeric entry for every numeric parameter

Improvements
- Stability and audio quality polished throughout

This is not a medical device. Biofeedback is for creative and self-observation purposes only.
