Health Disclaimer

Updated: February 2026

Important: Not a Medical Device

Echoelmusic is a creative audio-visual tool, NOT a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical advice.

Biofeedback Features

Echoelmusic uses biometric data (heart rate, HRV, breathing patterns) for creative and self-observation purposes only. These readings are approximate and should not be used for medical monitoring.

  • Do not rely on this app for health decisions
  • HRV and coherence metrics are for creative inspiration only
  • Consult a doctor before beginning any new breathing or relaxation practice

Safe Use Warning

Bio-reactive sound, rhythmic breath-pacing visuals, and animated light patterns are intense sensory experiences (a creative effect, not a therapeutic one). To use the app safely:

  • Do not use while driving or operating machinery or vehicles of any kind.
  • Do not use while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • If you are undergoing therapeutic treatment, coordinate any use with your healthcare provider, including medication timing.
  • Use only when seated or in a safe, stationary environment.

Photosensitivity Warning

Some visual effects include flashing lights, rapid color changes, and strobing patterns. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, a history of seizures, or are prone to light-induced discomfort, consult a physician before use. Stop using immediately if you experience discomfort.

Accessibility & Flash Safety

Echoelmusic respects your system Reduce Motion setting, and visual flash is capped at 3 Hz by design (W3C WCAG epilepsy guidance) — this limit applies to on-screen visuals and to Art-Net / sACN light output alike.

Hearing Safety

Prolonged exposure to loud audio can damage hearing. Use moderate volume levels. Take breaks during extended sessions. If you experience ringing in ears or discomfort, reduce volume immediately.

Lighting Output Safety

Echoelmusic can drive venue lighting over Art-Net and sACN — dimmer and colour output to your fixtures. Bio-reactive light changes are smoothly faded and capped at 3 Hz, but venue rigs can still be bright and fast-changing:

  • Keep flash and colour-change rates within safe limits for your audience, including anyone with photosensitivity.
  • Follow all local regulations and manufacturer guidance for the fixtures you connect.
  • Provide clear sightlines and safe intensity levels for the space.

For Coaches & Therapists: If you use Echoelmusic in professional practice, ensure clients understand this is a creative tool, not a therapeutic device. It does not replace clinical biofeedback equipment or professional treatment.

Emergency

If you experience any adverse reactions while using Echoelmusic, stop using the app immediately and seek medical attention if necessary.
