FAQ

Everything you need to know about Echoelmusic

General

What is Echoelmusic?
Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive generative music instrument that transforms your biometric signals (heart rate, HRV, breathing) into harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in real time, which you export as .wav and MIDI for your DAW. GPU bio-reactive visuals and Art-Net/sACN lighting output ship today; video and live broadcast are the wider vision, in active development. Coming to iPhone first, with iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro planned.
Which features are available at launch?
At launch Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive music instrument: bio-reactive synthesis (EchoelSynth), generative composition of harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in any key and BPM (EchoelGen), GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals in a movable window or fullscreen (EchoelVis, flash-safe), Art-Net and sACN lighting output (EchoelLux), export to .wav and MIDI for Ableton, Logic and FL Studio, MIDI 2.0/MPE (EchoelMIDI), biometrics (EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath via HealthKit, Bluetooth straps like Polar H10, and camera rPPG with a live control display), OSC and ADM-OSC sync (EchoelNet), an AUv3 plugin, and coherence / self-observation. Video capture & editing, live streaming, DMX-512 interfaces, projection mapping, on-device AI, spatial audio and Ableton Link are the wider vision — in active development, not in the app today. The Architecture page lists exact current status.
What makes it "bio-reactive"?
Bio-reactive means the software responds to your body in real time. EchoelBio captures your biometrics — HRV, heart rate, breath, and movement — and feeds them to the sound engine in real time. Your heart rate influences tempo and filter brightness, HRV modulates reverb and effects depth, breathing shapes the envelope, and your coherence level affects harmony, warmth, and saturation. Your body becomes the controller.
Do I need special hardware?
No — just your iPhone. The camera pulse works with no extra hardware: cover the rear camera + flash with a fingertip and Echoel reads your heartbeat (photoplethysmography) in real time. For the lowest latency and HRV ground-truth, pair a standard Bluetooth heart-rate device (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin/CooSpo straps, or a watch in HR-broadcast mode). An Apple Watch works via Apple Health (slightly higher latency). For MIDI, any class-compliant controller connects instantly.
Does it work with my Oura Ring or smartwatch?
Yes, via Apple Health — enable your device's Apple Health sync (Oura, Garmin, Fitbit, Apple Watch, etc.) and Echoel reads heart rate and HRV from there. One honest caveat about latency: the Oura Ring (incl. Ring 4) exposes no real-time Bluetooth to third-party apps — it pairs only with the Oura app, and its data reaches Echoel through Apple Health with a delay (not beat-to-beat). For a latency-free live experience, use the camera pulse or a standard Bluetooth heart-rate device — which is exactly what Oura itself recommends for live heart rate.
What are the EchoelTools?
Echoelmusic's engines are organized into unified tools. Some ship today; the rest are the wider vision, in active development:
  • EchoelSynth — Bio-reactive synthesis engines (DDSP, Modal, Cellular Automata)
  • EchoelGen — Generative composition: harmonic, melodic & rhythmic loops in any key and BPM
  • EchoelFX — Effects & analog emulations (EQ, Comp, Reverb, Delay, Tube, FET, VCA)
  • EchoelMIDI — MIDI 2.0, MPE, touch instruments, Audio→MIDI
  • EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath & LF/HF analysis (HealthKit, Bluetooth straps, camera rPPG)
  • EchoelNet — OSC & ADM-OSC immersive object output
  • EchoelVis — GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals: ten generative looks, flash-safe, floating window or fullscreen
  • EchoelLux — Art-Net + sACN lighting output over open standards (DMX-512 interfaces & smart home Planned)
  • EchoelMix Planned — Console mixer, multi-track recording, session management & metering
  • EchoelVid Planned — Video capture, NLE editor, multi-cam, ProRes, color grading
  • EchoelStage Planned — External displays, projection mapping & AirPlay
  • EchoelAI Planned — On-device CoreML intelligence & AI stem separation
Every tool talks to every other tool in real time.

Instruments & Synthesis

What synthesis engines are included?
All synthesis is unified inside EchoelSynth — one tool with multiple pluggable backends:
  • DDSP — Differentiable DSP (harmonic + noise model)
  • Modal — Physics-constrained modal resonator (bells, plates, strings, gongs)
  • Cellular Automata — Wolfram rules drive audio synthesis
Your biometrics compose harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops in any key and BPM — with an optional beat layer blending samples and physical-model drum synthesis — mapped directly to sound parameters in real time. Render to .wav and MIDI for your DAW.
What touch instruments are available?
The primary instrument is your body — and any external MIDI/MPE controller you plug in plays the built-in voices with per-note expression. In-app you play a drum-pad grid (velocity, accent, swing, per-pad samples), edit notes in a piano roll, and shape sounds in the patch editor. Dedicated touch instruments (chord pad, melody XY pad, multi-octave keyboard, strum pad) are on the roadmap, not in the app today.
Are there orchestral instruments?
Echoelmusic is a synthesizer, not a sample library. EchoelSynth offers naturally-voiced DDSP timbre presets — violin, cello, clarinet, oboe, flute, trumpet and more — plus physics-based modal resonators (bells, plates, strings, gongs). These are living synthesis models shaped by your body in real time, not multisampled orchestral sections. Full orchestral libraries with articulations are not a goal.

Effects & DSP

How many effects are there?
All effects are unified inside EchoelFX — one bio-reactive chain, built natively with zero external dependencies. Shipping today: reverb, tape-style delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, bitcrush, stereo widener and filter, plus LoFi characters (Underwater, Telephone, Cassette, Vinyl, Dream, Megaphone) and a mastering stage (EQ → compressor → limiter with true-peak trim). Body signals can modulate FX parameters live. Every effect is a real DSP algorithm — nothing is a placeholder.
What analog-inspired processing is included?
The saturation stage adds analog-style drive and warmth, the tape-style delay brings wow-and-flutter echo character, and the LoFi characters (Cassette, Vinyl, Telephone and more) give a take instant vintage texture. A dedicated suite of named analog emulations is on the roadmap, not in the app today — we only list what ships.

Composing & Music Creation

Does Echoelmusic compose music for me?
No — and that's by design. Echoelmusic is a creativity tool, not an AI that generates finished pieces. Your biometric data guides the musical parameters — tempo from your heart rate, harmonic variation from your HRV, dynamic expression from your breathing — but you are always the creator. We evolve your creativity, we don't replace it.
What composing tools are available?
EchoelGen composes for you from your body:
  • Generative loops — Harmonic, melodic and rhythmic loops composed in real time, in any key and BPM
  • Bio-reactive variation — The composition mutates with your bio-state: calm states produce ordered, settled phrases; higher arousal adds variation
  • Coherence & self-observation — Lock to a BPM and create, or simply breathe and watch the music respond
  • Export — Render any take to .wav and MIDI and drop it into Ableton, Logic or FL Studio

MIDI & Hardware

What MIDI features are supported?
All MIDI lives inside EchoelMIDI. MIDI 2.0-ready input (Universal MIDI Packets — parses both 1.0 and 2.0 traffic) with MPE for per-note expression, and a virtual “Echoelmusic” MIDI/MPE output any DAW can record (MIDI 1.0 over UMP today; native 2.0 out is on the roadmap). Any class-compliant MIDI controller works. Inter-app tempo sync and audio-to-MIDI are on the roadmap, not in the app today.
What about recording and export?
Today Echoelmusic exports your take to WAV (lossless) and MIDI — auto-named with artist, date, key and BPM — ready to drop into Ableton, Logic or FL Studio. A console mixer with multi-track recording (EchoelMix), plus FLAC/ALAC and video export (ProRes, H.264/H.265), are on the roadmap, not in the app today.
Does Echoelmusic work as a plugin and host?
Both. Echoelmusic ships as an AUv3 Audio Unit plugin — load it in Logic Pro, GarageBand, AUM, or any AUv3-compatible host and record its bio-reactive sound straight into your session. Echoelmusic can also act as a host for third-party AUv3 instruments and effects. VST3 and CLAP formats for macOS DAWs (Ableton Live, Bitwig, REAPER) are on the roadmap.

Visuals & Lighting

What visual modes are available?
GPU/Metal bio-reactive visuals ship today. EchoelVis renders ten generative looks — Rings, Chladni, Plasma, Water, Prism, Aurora, Lissajous, Depth Caustics, Oscilloscope and Fractal — driven live by your heart rate, coherence and breath, in a movable floating window or fullscreen for projection. Flash rate is capped under 3 Hz (WCAG epilepsy guidance) and Reduce Motion is honoured. Richer generative and AR worlds remain the wider vision, in active development.
Can I control lights and lasers?
Yes — Art-Net and sACN (E1.31) lighting output ship today. Your heartbeat can drive professional fixtures (moving heads, LED bars) natively over open network standards — no vendor SDK, with flash safety built in. Echoelmusic also speaks OSC and ADM-OSC live, so your body can drive a TouchDesigner or Resolume rig in real time. On the roadmap: DMX-512 interfaces, smart-home lights, and EchoelStage (external displays via USB-C/HDMI/AirPlay, projection mapping, cue system). Today you can mirror the fullscreen visual to a projector via AirPlay.

Video & Streaming

What video features are included?
Video editing is on the roadmap, not in the app today. Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive music instrument first. EchoelVid — a full NLE with timeline, multi-cam, ProRes, and color grading — is a planned future direction. What ships today is audio: your heartbeat composes music you can export as WAV and MIDI to Ableton, FL Studio, or any DAW.
Can I stream live?
Live streaming (RTMP) is on the roadmap, not shipping yet. Today you create and export. For immersive and stage work, Echoelmusic already speaks open standards live — OSC, ADM-OSC (immersive audio objects), Art-Net and sACN (stage lighting) — so a performer's body can drive a TouchDesigner / Resolume / lighting rig in real time.

Intelligence

What AI features does Echoelmusic have?
On-device AI is on the roadmap, not in the app today. The vision for EchoelAI is entirely on-device via CoreML — AI stem separation to isolate vocals, bass and instruments, with no cloud and full privacy. In active development.

Collaboration & Immersive

Can I collaborate with others?
Yes — EchoelNet provides OSC (Open Sound Control) for bidirectional communication with DAWs, VJ software, and custom applications, plus a virtual MIDI/MPE source any DAW on the network can record live. Ableton Link tempo sync is on the roadmap.
What about immersive and spatial setups?
Echoel is iPhone-first today. For immersive and spatial work it acts as a bio-reactive object source: the body (breath, coherence, HRV, motion) drives an audio object's position and gain over ADM-OSC — the open Audio Definition Model over Open Sound Control standard — into object-based renderers such as Adamson FletcherMachine, L-ISA and d&b Soundscape. No vendor SDK; open standards only. A dedicated Vision Pro app, on-device immersive scenes and other Apple platforms are on the roadmap, not yet shipped — see the Architecture page for what is live today versus planned.

Health & Privacy

Is this a medical device?
No. Echoelmusic is a creative tool, not a medical device. Biofeedback features (heart rate, HRV, coherence scores) are for creative and self-observation purposes only. They are not clinically validated measurements. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice. See our Health Disclaimer.
Photosensitivity warnings?
Some visual effects include flashing lights, rapid color changes, and laser patterns. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or seizure conditions, consult a physician before use. Accessibility options can reduce or disable motion and flashing effects.
Is my data safe?
All biometric data is processed locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Your device's own on-device Data Protection encrypts local storage. See our Privacy Policy and Security page.

Accessibility

Is Echoelmusic accessible?
We build with accessibility in mind. Today the app provides VoiceOver labels on the primary controls, reduced-motion support, a dark high-contrast interface, and a safety cap that keeps any visual movement at or below 3 Hz (W3C WCAG epilepsy guidance). WCAG 2.2 AA is our target as the app grows; broader accessibility modes, color-vision options and additional languages are planned, not in the app today. The app currently ships in English. See our Accessibility page.
