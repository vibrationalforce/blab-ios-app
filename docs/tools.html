What Echoel Does

Your body makes the music. You take it into your DAW. On-device, accessible, free.

Echoel turns your heartbeat, breath and heart-rate variability into musical starting material — pads, chords, leads, arps and atmospheres across many genres — then hands it to your studio over the interfaces you already use. No cloud. No account. No lock-in.

The Body Is the Instrument

Heart rate · HRV · breath · coherence — read live, explained in plain language.

Echoel reads your physiology from the iPhone camera (finger on the lens) or any standard Bluetooth heart-rate strap (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin, CooSpo…). Heart rate sets energy and tempo, HRV opens or closes the timbre, breath shapes phrasing, coherence decides whether the music is calm and spacious or busy. Every number is shown large and legible — tap any value to learn what it means. Science-first: this is for music and self-observation, not medical diagnosis.

Real sensors

Camera rPPG (on-device), universal BLE heart-rate (0x180D); Apple Health (Watch, ring) as a delayed source. RMSSD / SDNN / coherence computed on device.

Explained, not mystified

Tap HR, HRV or coherence for a clear definition. No esoteric claims, no “healing frequencies”.

Bio-Driven Sound & Loops

Pads, chords, leads, arps — generated in key, ready to cut into loops.

Pick a genre and a key; your body composes in-key material and you shape it live in the patch editor — there is no note grid to draw in, on purpose. Nineteen curated genres across 57 scales — eight on the ambient/meditative shelf (Self-Observation, Deep Ambient, Drift, Contemplation, Vaporwave, Sci-Fi, Still Drone, Ambient Pulse), ten techno, house and trance (Dub Techno, Acid Techno, Deep House, Deep Tech, Dark Minimal, Psy Prog House, Tech House, Minimal Techno, Detroit Techno, Uplifting Trance), plus Classical — all harmonic layers, the starting basis for a professional production. Lock to a BPM for the studio, or stay sync-free for ambient self-observation.

Synthesis engines

A DDSP harmonic-plus-noise voice, a polyphonic pad/lead and a dedicated sub-bass — vDSP-accelerated, zero external dependencies. Design and save your own patches.

Studio precision

BPM and concert pitch (Kammerton, freely settable 380.00–500.00 Hz) to two decimals. Tempo-synced effects and LFOs in ms · Hz · samples, with dotted and triplet.

Effect characters

One-tap production colors — Underwater, Telephone, Cassette, Vinyl, Dream, Megaphone — or each genre’s own signature space (dub delay, vapor chorus, psy roll). Clean resets to dry.

Loops

Cut to 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64 bars, sample-accurate. Tight grid or humanized — you decide.

One Instrument, Many Dimensions

The same body that plays sound also plays space, light and vibration — in real time, over open standards.

Echoel isn’t a stack of separate tools — it’s one instrument played by the body. Beyond sound, your pulse drives several real output dimensions at once, each over an open protocol so it plugs into the gear you already own. This is what “multidimensional” means here, today.

Space — spatial-audio object

Your body streams as an immersive audio object over ADM-OSC into object-based renderers (L-ISA, d&b Soundscape, FletcherMachine): breath→azimuth, coherence→distance, HRV→elevation. On-device head-tracked binaural for headphones is planned, not in the app today.

Light — fixtures from your pulse

Drive real lighting over Art-Net and sACN — the heartbeat that shapes the sound shapes the room. How the two protocols differ, and what a phone can send.

Vibration — felt bass / LFE

A dedicated sub-bass one octave below the body’s bass line, with a push-to-feel level — felt on a sub, in headphones, or as Core Haptics. The physical, body-sonic dimension.

Immersive 360° Planned

Multichannel render (5.1 / 7.1.4), true LFE, and a Vision Pro 360° scene are on the roadmap — the spatial object source already works today.

Into Your Studio — Your Way

WAV and MIDI file export · audio out · MIDI in and note out · OSC. The interfaces you already use.

However you produce, Echoel fits the workflow you already have:

MIDI 2.0-ready in

Universal MIDI Packets, parsing both MIDI 1.0 and 2.0. Notes and pitch bend play the built-in performer voice today — one monophonic voice, which takes priority over the breath envelope. Slide (CC 74) opens that voice’s filter and Press (channel pressure) swells it. Air-CC (CC 21–31) is parsed and carried on the control bus, but no voice consumes it. Full MPE zone handling and per-note pressure are on the roadmap.

Live MIDI out

A virtual “Echoelmusic” MIDI 1.0 source any DAW records live, created once you enable a MIDI route in the routing panel — carrying MPE output once you turn on the two MPE switches beside it. Native MIDI 2.0 out and Ableton Link tempo sync are on the roadmap.

Export your take

WAV — drop the file straight into FL Studio, Logic or Ableton and keep working. Files are auto-named with artist · date · key · BPM · Kammerton, so a take lands in your DAW already labeled.

OSC / ADM-OSC data out

Stream live bio values (heart, breath, HRV, coherence) over OSC to any software, and the body as an immersive audio object over ADM-OSC. Open standards — no SDK lock-in.

For Everyone, Everywhere

A worldwide, inclusive, accessible biofeedback-music experience.

Music-making is still out of reach for many disabled, blind, Deaf and neurodivergent people. Echoel is built the other way round: the body is the controller, so making sound needs no fine motor skill, no notation, and no screen-reading of tiny dials.

VoiceOver-first

Labelled controls and bio readouts, designed to be played without turning VoiceOver off.

Legibility & motion safety

Large legible numbers, high contrast, reduced-motion support, and a hard 3 Hz flash ceiling (WCAG epilepsy safety).

Private & free

Everything runs on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no subscription — v1.0 is completely free.

On the Roadmap Planned

Honest about what isn’t here yet.

Echoel’s focus is sound and data — and today that already includes GPU bio-reactive visuals and Art-Net / sACN lighting output. DMX-512 interfaces, projection mapping / stage output and on-device AI are part of the wider vision but are not in the app today — they arrive only when they meet the same on-device, no-lock-in bar. Video editing and live streaming (RTMP) are not planned at all: video editing was built and removed in July 2026, and RTMP was never built — the publisher is a compile-guarded scaffold and HaishinKit is not a dependency.
