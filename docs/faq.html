FAQ

Everything you need to know about Echoelmusic

General

What is Echoelmusic?
Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive creative platform that transforms your biometric signals (heart rate, HRV, breathing) into music, visuals, and lighting in real-time. Coming to iPhone first, with iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro planned.
Which features are available at launch?
At launch Echoelmusic is a bio-reactive performance instrument: synthesis (EchoelSynth), effects & mastering (EchoelFX), mixing & recording (EchoelMix), the step sequencer (EchoelSeq), MIDI 2.0/MPE (EchoelMIDI), biometrics (EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath, Polar H10), 120fps visuals + chroma key (EchoelVis), short-form video export (EchoelVid), OSC sync (EchoelNet), and an AUv3 plugin. Lighting (EchoelLux), stage output (EchoelStage), on-device AI (EchoelAI), plus camera rPPG, face tracking, ProRes/NLE video, projection mapping, spatial audio and Ableton Link are on the roadmap. The Architecture page lists exact current status.
What makes it "bio-reactive"?
Bio-reactive means the software responds to your body in real time. EchoelBio captures your biometrics — HRV, heart rate, breath, face tracking, and movement — and feeds them to every other tool via EchoelCreativeWorkspace. Your heart rate influences tempo and filter brightness, HRV modulates reverb and effects depth, breathing shapes delay and spatial audio, and your coherence level affects harmony, warmth, and saturation. Your body becomes the controller.
Do I need special hardware?
For basic use, just your iPhone or iPad. Bio-reactive features work with your iPhone's front camera (face tracking for pulse and expression) or an Apple Watch / Bluetooth heart rate monitor for deeper biometric data. For lighting, Echoelmusic controls smart home lights, DMX fixtures, and Art-Net networks — from your living room to the stage. For MIDI, any class-compliant controller connects instantly.
What are the EchoelTools?
All of Echoelmusic's engines are consolidated into 12 unified tools, connected via EchoelCreativeWorkspace:
  • EchoelSynth — Multiple synthesis engines (DDSP, Modal, Cellular Automata, Sampler, EchoelBeat, Breakbeat Chopper, Spectral Morph)
  • EchoelFX — Effects & analog emulations (EQ, Comp, Reverb, Delay, Tube, FET, VCA)
  • EchoelMix — Console mixer, multi-track recording, session management & metering
  • EchoelSeq — Step sequencer, pattern bank, Euclidean rhythms & automation
  • EchoelMIDI — MIDI 2.0, MPE, touch instruments (keys, pads, drums, strum, Audio→MIDI)
  • EchoelBio — HRV, heart rate, breath, face tracking (ARKit) & LF/HF analysis
  • EchoelVis — GPU-accelerated visuals (10+ modes, Metal 120fps, Hilbert bio-mapping)
  • EchoelVid — Video capture, NLE editor, multi-cam, ProRes, color grading
  • EchoelLux — DMX 512, Art-Net, smart home lighting & cue system
  • EchoelStage — External displays, projection mapping & AirPlay
  • EchoelNet — OSC & Ableton Link networking
  • EchoelAI — On-device CoreML intelligence & AI stem separation
Fewer tools, more power. Every tool talks to every other tool in real time.

Instruments & Synthesis

What synthesis engines are included?
All synthesis is unified inside EchoelSynth — one tool with multiple pluggable backends:
  • DDSP — Differentiable DSP (harmonic + noise model, ML-powered)
  • Modal — Physics-constrained modal resonator (bells, plates, strings, gongs)
  • Cellular Automata — Wolfram rules drive audio synthesis
  • Sampler — Multi-zone sample playback with velocity layers
  • EchoelBeat — Classic drum machine + bass synth
  • Breakbeat Chopper — Jungle/DnB slice engine with transient detection
Plus factory presets across multiple categories and bio-reactive synthesis that maps your biometrics directly to sound parameters in real time. All connected via EchoelCreativeWorkspace.
What touch instruments are available?
  • Chord Pad — 11 chord types, 13 scales, strum & arpeggio modes
  • Drum Pad — 16-pad grid with 6 kits (Acoustic, Electronic, EchoelBeat, TR-909, Hip Hop, Percussion)
  • Melody XY Pad — Continuous 2D pitch/timbre control with scale quantization
  • Keyboard — Multi-octave piano with velocity & pitch bend
  • Strum Pad — Guitar-style strumming with full chord support
All touch instruments support MIDI 2.0 with MPE (per-note expression).
Are there orchestral instruments?
Yes. Orchestral instruments across full sections — Strings (Violins I/II, Violas, Cellos, Basses), Brass (Trumpets, French Horns, Trombones, Tuba), Woodwinds (Flutes, Oboes, Clarinets, Bassoons), Choir (SATB), Keyboards (Grand Piano, Concert Harp, Celesta), and Percussion (Timpani). Many articulation types from legato and spiccato to col legno, flutter tongue, and overblown.

Effects & DSP

How many effects are there?
All effects are unified inside EchoelFX — one tool with a full range of effect types and slot-based inserts. Includes EQ, compressor, reverb, delay, modulation, drive, limiter, gate, de-esser, stereo imaging, pitch correction, analog console saturation, and full analog emulations (VCA, Tube EQ, FET, VariMu, Opto). All built natively with zero external dependencies. Every effect has real DSP algorithms.
What analog-inspired processing is included?
Our EchoelWarmth engine offers 9 analog-inspired processing modes — each modeled after the character of classic studio hardware, with our own Echoelmusic identity:
  • EchoelGlue — Punchy VCA bus compression
  • EchoelThrust — Aggressive punch with tone control
  • EchoelSilk — Warm transformer saturation with inductor EQ
  • EchoelAir — Passive tube EQ with the famous boost/cut trick
  • EchoelVelvet — Smooth variable-mu tube limiting
  • EchoelOpto — Program-dependent optical compression
  • EchoelBite — Fast FET limiting with all-buttons mode
  • EchoelDream — Ultra-smooth tube mastering
  • EchoelWarmth — Feedback compression with British character
Plus EchoelVibe creative effects: EchoelPunish saturation (5 drive modes), EchoelTime delay (8 echo styles), and EchoelMorph pitch/formant shifting with robot mode.

Composing & Music Creation

Does Echoelmusic compose music for me?
No — and that's by design. Echoelmusic is a creativity tool, not an AI that generates finished pieces. Your biometric data guides the musical parameters — tempo from your heart rate, harmonic variation from your HRV, dynamic expression from your breathing — but you are always the creator. We evolve your creativity, we don't replace it.
What composing tools are available?
EchoelSeq provides the sequencer and automation layer:
  • Step Sequencer — 8-track, variable steps with probability, velocity per step, large pattern bank
  • Euclidean Rhythms — Algorithmically generated patterns with polyrhythm and swing
  • Automation — Record and draw automation for any parameter with curves, ramps, and bio-reactive modulation
  • Bio-Reactive Patterns — Patterns mutate based on your bio-state. Calm states produce ordered rhythms, stress creates variation.

MIDI & Hardware

What MIDI features are supported?
All MIDI lives inside EchoelMIDI. Full MIDI 2.0 with Universal MIDI Packets (32-bit resolution), MPE (MIDI Polyphonic Expression) for per-note control, and inter-app tempo sync for beat-locked sessions with other music apps and devices. Any class-compliant MIDI controller works. Controllers with RGB LED grids get dedicated bio-reactive feedback patterns. Real-time Audio→MIDI pitch detection lets you sing or play any instrument to generate MIDI notes.
What about recording and export?
EchoelMix handles recording and session management. Export formats including WAV, FLAC, AAC, MP3, ProRes, and H.264/H.265. Export presets for streaming services, social media, and broadcast. Multi-track recording with punch-in, loop recording, and automatic take management.
Does Echoelmusic work as a plugin and host?
Both. All 12 Echoel* tools are available as AUv3 Audio Unit plugins. Load them in Logic Pro, GarageBand, AUM, or any AUv3-compatible host. On macOS, VST3 and CLAP formats are also supported for use in Ableton Live, Bitwig, REAPER, and other DAWs. Echoelmusic itself is also a host — load third-party AUv3 instruments and effects directly into the bio-reactive signal chain.

Visuals & Lighting

What visual modes are available?
All visuals live inside EchoelVis — GPU-accelerated Metal rendering at 120fps. 10+ modes: Waveform, Spectrum, Particles, Hilbert Map, Bio Graph, Flow Field, Kaleidoscope, Nebula, Generative Worlds, and AR Worlds. All respond to your audio and biometrics in real time via Hilbert curve bio-mapping.
Can I control lights and lasers?
Yes — lighting lives inside EchoelLux and stage output inside EchoelStage. From your living room to the stage. Smart home lights respond to your biometrics and music in real time. DMX 512 and Art-Net 4 control professional fixtures — moving heads, LED bars, and strobes. External displays via USB-C/HDMI/AirPlay, projection mapping with edge blending and warping. A professional cue system syncs all lighting to the beat grid.

Video & Streaming

What video features are included?
Video lives inside EchoelVid — a full NLE editor with timeline, razor, and keyframe animation, multi-cam switching with stabilization, Apple ProRes recording, professional color grading with LUTs, curves, and color wheels, Metal GPU shader pipeline, and multi-layer compositing with blend modes.
Can I stream live?
Yes. Professional RTMP streaming with complete protocol handshake, multi-platform support (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok), adaptive bitrate from 1.5 Mbps to 50 Mbps, H.264 hardware encoding, and real-time quality monitoring.

Intelligence

What AI features does Echoelmusic have?
All intelligence lives inside EchoelAI — entirely on-device via CoreML. AI stem separation isolates vocals, drums, bass, and instruments. No cloud, no latency, fully private.

Health & Privacy

Is this a medical device?
No. Echoelmusic is a creative tool, not a medical device. Biofeedback features (heart rate, HRV, coherence scores) are for creative and self-observation purposes only. They are not clinically validated measurements. Always consult healthcare professionals for medical advice. See our Health Disclaimer.
Photosensitivity warnings?
Some visual effects include flashing lights, rapid color changes, and laser patterns. If you have photosensitive epilepsy or seizure conditions, consult a physician before use. Accessibility options can reduce or disable motion and flashing effects.
Is my data safe?
All biometric data is processed locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Your device's own on-device Data Protection encrypts local storage. See our Privacy Policy and Security page.

Accessibility

Is Echoelmusic accessible?
Yes. WCAG 2.2 AA as our accessibility target, with 6 accessibility modes: Standard, Vision Assist (VoiceOver, large text, high contrast), Hearing Assist (visual alerts, captions), Motor Assist (large targets, voice control, switch access), Cognitive Assist (simplified UI), and Full Accessibility. 5 color blindness modes using the Brettel algorithm. Reduced motion support throughout. 37 languages with RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew. See our Accessibility page.
