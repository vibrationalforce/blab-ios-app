# Echoel — Deep-Research Marketing Analysis (2026-06-21)

PIPELINE-only (marketing). Brand-clean per `echoel-marketing` guardrails + `memory/vision.md`.
Claims grounded in `docs/dev/FEATURE_MATRIX.md` (code = truth). NO wellness/esoteric/overclaim.
("Super Intelligence" etc. = banned; energy yes, copy no.)

## 0) TL;DR — the wedge (one sentence)
**Echoel is the only iPhone *instrument* where the body (heart, breath, coherence) is the
controller for music AND its multidimensional output — visuals, light (Art-Net/sACN),
spatial (ADM-OSC), MIDI/MPE — fully on-device, over open standards, no account, no cloud.**
The market splits cleanly into three camps and **nobody sits in the middle where Echoel is.**

## 1) Market landscape (deep research, 3 arenas)

**A. Mobile DAW / AUv3 hosts (the "workflow" camp)** — Loopy Pro, AUM, Drambo, Cubasis 3,
FL Studio Mobile (~$20+ one-time, AUv3 now but "doesn't see all AUv3 plugins" per users),
GarageBand (free). Deep, mature, *no body input, no light/spatial output.* Community lives on
the Audiobus / Loopy Pro forum (forum.audiob.us). → Echoel is NOT trying to out-DAW these;
it hosts AUv3 (roadmap) but its reason-to-exist is the bio + multidimensional spine.

**B. Bio / HRV apps (the "wellness" camp)** — HRV4Biofeedback, eSense Pulse, Mindbreath.
Camera/strap HRV → **breathing pacers, scores, nature sounds, charts.** Academic work confirms
HRV→generative-music is promising but **commercial apps stay on wellness viz, not a creative
instrument.** This is exactly the white space — and the camp Echoel must NOT be mistaken for
(science-first modulation, never therapy/healing).

**C. Immersive AV / light control (the "output" camp)** — Photon (iPad DMX console),
StageLight (Art-Net+OSC), TouchOSC/Resolume (control surfaces). These are **manual consoles**;
Echoel is the **bio-reactive *source*** that drives them (the object source, not the renderer).

**The gap:** A=workflow without body; B=body without instrument; C=output without a generative
source. **Echoel = body → instrument → multidimensional output, on-device, open standards.**
No competitor occupies all three. That intersection IS the moat.

## 2) Positioning (brand-clean)
- **One-liner:** *"Your body plays the music — and the light, the space, the visuals."*
- **Category line:** "Echoel — Physical Computing · Biofeedback · Multimedial & Multidimensional."
- **Tagline / brand:** *Create From Within.* "The multidimensional production instrument."
- **Proof, not promise:** show the number (HR/HRV/coherence), show the colour tracking the
  melody, show OSC/Art-Net/ADM going out. Never "heal/calm/therapy", never "viral/super-AI".
- **Anti-positioning (say what we're NOT):** not a wellness app, not a HeartMath clone, not
  another AUv3 host — *an instrument the body plays.*

## 3) ASO (App Store) — grounded in what SHIPS
- **App name (≤30):** `Echoel — Bio Music Instrument`
- **Subtitle (≤30):** `Your heartbeat plays music`
- **Keywords (≤100, no spaces):**
  `biofeedback,HRV,generative,synth,sequencer,AUv3,MIDI,MPE,OSC,DMX,artnet,heart,breath,coherence,visuals,light,immersive,modular`
- **Screens (order = the wedge):** 1) live BioStrip numbers + "your heartbeat composes" ·
  2) Music → colour (chord drives the visual) · 3) immersive full-screen visual reacting ·
  4) Arrange/Clips timeline (it's a real instrument) · 5) Light + OSC/ADM out = open standards ·
  6) "On-device. Private. No account." (the 2026 trust wedge).
- **Do NOT keyword/screenshot:** streaming, video editing, multitrack (roadmap — would be
  overclaim + rejection risk).

## 4) Channels & launch (solo founder, near-zero budget)
1. **Audiobus / Loopy Pro forum (forum.audiob.us)** — the center of gravity for iOS music.
   A genuine dev-thread ("bio-reactive instrument, open standards") = highest-ROI first move.
2. **r/iPadMusic, r/AudioProductionDeals, r/synthesizers** — short demo clips.
3. **YouTube/TikTok/IG Reels — the demo IS the ad:** 15–30 s "heartbeat → melody → colour →
   light" loop. This is unfakeable and no competitor can show it. (Content production via the
   `echoel-marketing` pipeline — never an in-app "viral" feature.)
4. **AUv3 / iOS-music YouTubers** (the review circuit) once AUv3-host ships.
5. **Open-standards / immersive niches** — ADM-OSC (L-ISA/d&b), Art-Net/QLC+, TouchDesigner
   crowds: "free bio object source" is a credible, un-hyped hook.
6. **Accessibility angle** — body-as-controller + VoiceOver/WCAG is a real, press-worthy story.

## 5) Pricing (recommendation — needs founder sign-off via Council/vision-gate)
- Vision says **on-device, private, free.** Recommend: **free core** (bio compose + synth +
  visual) as the irresistible hook → optional **one-time "Studio/Pro unlock" IAP**
  (non-subscription) for pro I/O (OSC/ADM/Art-Net/MIDI-out, AUv3 host when it lands).
- **Avoid subscription** — off-brand for an instrument, and "monthly biofeedback" reeks of the
  wellness camp we reject. One-time unlock fits anti-lock-in / no-SaaS ethos.
- Competitor anchors: FL Mobile ~$20 one-time; Cubasis/Loopy Pro paid; HRV apps often $5–10/mo
  subscription (we deliberately don't).

## 6) Honest-claims ledger (do / don't)
- ✅ Say: bio-reactive generative music; camera rPPG + BLE HR + HealthKit; on-device & private;
  Art-Net/sACN/ADM-OSC/OSC/MPE out; immersive Metal visual reacts to music + body; AUv3 plugin
  + Widget shipped.
- ⛔ Don't say (roadmap): live streaming/RTMP, video capture/edit, multitrack, AUv3 *hosting*,
  "viral", "wellness/therapy/healing", "super-AI". (Per FEATURE_MATRIX "Absent".)
