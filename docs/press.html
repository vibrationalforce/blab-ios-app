Press Kit

Facts, boilerplate and assets for writing about Echoelmusic

One-liner

Echoelmusic is the first bio-reactive performance instrument: your heartbeat and breath compose live, professional generative music and visuals — free, private, on your iPhone.

Boilerplate (short)

Echoelmusic turns the body into a musical instrument. The iPhone app reads heart rate, heart-rate variability and breath — from a fingertip on the camera, any standard Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or Apple Health — and composes live generative music with immersive, flash-safe visuals. Everything is processed on the device: no account, no ads, no tracking. Echoelmusic is built in Hamburg by artist and developer Echoel (Michael Terbuyken).

Boilerplate (long)

Echoelmusic — Physical Computing · Biofeedback · Multimedial & Multidimensional — is an immersive, iPhone-first instrument and production platform where the body drives sound, image and light in real time. A fingertip on the camera measures the pulse (photoplethysmography); research-grade frequency analysis derives heart-rate-variability coherence; one tap generates music in the player's own key across sixteen curated genres. Under the hood is a polyphonic synthesizer with a live patch editor, 57 scales, a production FX chain, and stamped WAV export straight into Ableton, Logic or FL Studio.

For stage and installation work, Echoelmusic speaks open standards only: MIDI input, OSC and ADM-OSC object streams for immersive audio rigs, and Art-Net/sACN for stage lighting — no vendor lock-in. Biosignals are treated as a science-based modulation source, presented numbers-first for self-observation, never as medical diagnosis; visual flash rates are capped at 3 Hz per W3C WCAG guidance. The app is free, with no account, no ads, and no data collection.

What ships today

  • Pulse from the camera (fingertip photoplethysmography), any standard Bluetooth heart-rate strap (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin and more), or Apple Health — plus an instant demo mode.
  • Real HRV coherence from research-grade frequency analysis (Lomb-Scargle, Welch) — numbers first, always visible.
  • One-tap generative composition: sixteen curated genres, 57 scales, your key, concert pitch A4 432–444 Hz, tempo locked or flowing with the heart.
  • A real instrument: polyphonic synth with a live patch editor and preset library, sub-bass voice, loop cutter, production FX chain.
  • Studio handoff: stamped WAV export (artist · date · key · BPM · tuning), plus MIDI input from an external controller (notes, pitch bend, CC 74).
  • Immersive GPU visuals, flash-safe (≤3 Hz) and Reduce-Motion aware, recordable as share-ready MP4 clips.
  • Open-standard stage I/O: OSC, ADM-OSC immersive objects, Art-Net and sACN lighting.
  • Nearby session sharing between two iPhones (peer-to-peer, no server).

Deliberately not in Echoelmusic: video editing, multitrack recording, an arrangement timeline, live broadcast, and Audio Unit (AUv3) plugin hosting. Several of those were built and then removed in July 2026 — Echoelmusic is an instrument, not a workstation, and every one of them was a second product to maintain. Status is always mirrored honestly on the Architecture page.

Story angles

  • The body as controller: a musical instrument you play with your heartbeat — measured with nothing but the iPhone camera.
  • Science, not wellness: peer-reviewed bio-signal processing (HRV resonance research) as a creative modulation source, presented for self-observation, never as therapy.
  • Open standards on stage: one iPhone driving immersive audio objects (ADM-OSC) and stage lights (Art-Net/sACN) with no vendor SDK.
  • Privacy as a feature: a generative music app with no account, no cloud and a truthful "Data Not Collected" label.
  • Solo-built in Hamburg: an independent artist-developer shipping a full instrument and light controller as one app.
