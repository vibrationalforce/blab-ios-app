Echoel

Michael Terbuyken · Music Producer & Sound Artist · Hamburg

Music producer, sound artist and inventor of walk-in vibrating water, light and sound installations. Based in Hamburg. Creating interactive multisensory installations, video, visuals, light and musical performance since 2006 — most recently a vibration pontons and sound mirror workshop at Burgbeben STRNBRG 2024, created together with Anna.Elisie as Loewe Immerlieb. Active across multiple music projects with the Tropical Drones collective, and creator of Echoelmusic — a bio-reactive performance instrument.
