Echoel is a bio-reactive performance instrument. Your heart rate, heart-rate variability and breath drive sound, image and light in real time — the body becomes the controller.

Built for performance, installation, content and studio work — on iPhone.


THE BODY AS CONTROLLER

Connect Apple Watch, a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or use the iPhone camera (rPPG) to read your pulse:

- Heart rate shapes tempo and vibrato
- HRV and coherence shape brightness, harmonicity and texture
- Breath shapes the amplitude envelope and space
- Motion drives onsets and dynamics

No two takes are the same.


CREATE

- Bio-reactive synthesis engine (additive DDSP, modal, cellular, sampler) — vDSP-accelerated, no external dependencies
- Design and save your own patches
- Composition: pick a genre and a key; generate in-key material you keep editing in a real piano roll
- Concert pitch (A4 / Kammerton) set exactly to the cent; lock a BPM for tight loops or stay sync-free
- 16-step, 8-track beat maker and sampler with velocity, accent, swing and per-pad sample import
- Effects: reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, harmonizer and more
- Immersive visual: the notes you play become colour in real time (perceptual pitch-to-hue), reacting to the music and your body
- Retroactive capture: keep the loop you just played
- Export audio (WAV) and MIDI (.mid) for your DAW


CONNECT

- MIDI / MPE in and out
- OSC output for live rigs and visuals
- ADM-OSC immersive object output
- Art-Net native light output for installations and stage


PRIVACY

Your biometrics stay on your device — unless you explicitly enable OSC or ADM output, which sends live bio values to devices you choose on your own network. No accounts required, no ads, no tracking. What you create is yours.


ACCESSIBILITY

Designed accessibility-first: VoiceOver, large legible bio readouts, exact numeric entry for every parameter, and a 3 Hz visual flash cap for photosensitive safety.
