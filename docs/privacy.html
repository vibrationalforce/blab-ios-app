Privacy Policy

Updated: February 2026

Summary: No accounts. No tracking. No ads. Your data stays on your device.

Our Approach

Echoelmusic is built with privacy as a core principle, not an afterthought. We believe your biometric data, creative work, and personal health information belong to you alone.

Key principles:

  • No user accounts or authentication systems
  • No cloud storage of personal or biometric data
  • No tracking, analytics, or advertising networks
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • All processing happens on your device

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account information
  • Biometric data: Heart rate, HRV, breathing patterns, or any health metrics
  • Usage analytics: How you use the app, which features you access, or session duration
  • Device information: Device IDs, advertising IDs, or hardware specifications
  • Location data: GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi networks, or IP addresses
  • Audio recordings: Microphone input, voice data, or recorded audio
  • Visual data: Camera frames, photos, or video
  • Creative content: Your music, videos, or artistic creations

Data Processing

All data processing occurs locally on your device:

Biometric Data: Heart rate variability (HRV), heart rate, and breathing patterns from Apple Watch or HealthKit are processed in real-time on your device to influence audio, visuals, and lighting. This data never leaves your device and is not stored.

Audio Input: Microphone input for music production, voice synthesis, and audio analysis is processed locally. No audio is uploaded to servers or shared with third parties.

Camera (pulse / rPPG): When you start a camera pulse reading, the rear camera with the torch reads subtle color changes in your fingertip to estimate heart rate (photoplethysmography). Frames are analyzed in real time on-device and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. There is no face tracking, AR, or video recording.

Sensors: Bluetooth heart-rate straps are read on-device to drive the instrument. This stays on your device unless you explicitly switch on local-network output (OSC / ADM-OSC / Art-Net / sACN / MIDI) or nearby session sharing — see those sections below for exactly which values then leave.

HealthKit Data

Echoelmusic integrates with Apple HealthKit to access heart rate and HRV data for bio-reactive features. This integration is governed by Apple's strict privacy requirements:

  • HealthKit data is never shared with third parties
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising
  • HealthKit data never leaves your device
  • You control which health metrics Echoelmusic can access
  • You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings

We use HealthKit data solely to create bio-reactive audio-visual experiences in real-time. The data is read, processed, and immediately discarded—it is never stored.

Camera & Sensors

Echoelmusic requests camera and sensor permissions for the following features:

Camera Access: Used only to estimate your pulse from a fingertip on the rear camera (rPPG). No photos or video are recorded, and nothing is uploaded. No face tracking or AR.

Microphone Access: Used for voice-to-sound synthesis, pitch detection, and sound analysis. Audio is processed locally; any recording you make for export stays on your device.

You can deny or revoke these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Denying permissions will disable related features but the app will continue to function.

Local Network (OSC / Art-Net / MIDI)

If you explicitly enable it, Echoel can send one-way control data over your local network or to connected gear:

  • OSC and ADM-OSC messages, Art-Net / sACN lighting, and MIDI you initiate
  • This stream carries live biosignal values, not only control data. Over OSC it can include heart rate, HRV (RMSSD, SDNN, pNN50), breathing rate and phase, and coherence — that is what makes lights and other software react to your body
  • Only measurements Echoel takes itself are sent — the iPhone camera, a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or the demo generator. Values read from Apple Health or Apple Watch are never sent; they stay on your device
  • It is plain UDP on your local network, so it is not encrypted — treat that network as you would any studio or stage LAN
  • Nothing is sent to our servers (we operate none)
  • You choose the target device/address; it stays on your network

Nearby Session Sharing (peer-to-peer)

Echoelmusic can share a live session with someone next to you, device to device. It is off until you start or accept a session, and there is no server involved — the connection runs directly between the two devices over Apple's MultipeerConnectivity.

While a session is active, these values are sent to the other person's device about twice per second:

  • Your heart rate, coherence, normalized HRV and breathing rate
  • The display name you choose for the session

The other person sees those numbers live. As with local-network output, only measurements Echoel takes itself are shared — values read from Apple Health or Apple Watch are excluded by the same rule. Nothing is recorded, and nothing reaches us. Leaving the session ends the transmission.

Third-Party Services

Echoelmusic does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Facebook SDK or Pixel
  • Advertising networks (AdMob, etc.)
  • Crash reporting services that collect personal data
  • Social media login or sharing SDKs

If you choose to share your creations externally (e.g., exporting to Files, sharing via social media), those services' privacy policies apply.

Children's Privacy

Echoelmusic does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is designed to function entirely offline with no data collection, making it safe for all ages.

Parents and guardians should be aware that the app uses biometric sensors (heart rate) for creative expression. Data is processed locally and never sent to us — we operate no servers. It leaves the device only if someone switches on one of the outputs described above (local-network OSC/lighting, or nearby session sharing), each of which is off until enabled and sends only to a destination you pick.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect data, there is no data retention. Your creative projects, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, all local data is removed from your device.

To completely remove all data:

  • Delete the Echoelmusic app from your device
  • If you backed up projects to iCloud Drive or Files, delete them manually
  • Revoke HealthKit permissions in iOS Settings (optional)

Who Is Responsible

Because heart-rate, HRV, breathing and camera-pulse data are processed only on your device and we never receive them, we are not a "controller" of that data under the GDPR — you process it locally yourself. Where such bio-data is a special category (Art. 9 GDPR), the legal basis for the on-device use is your explicit consent, given through the iOS permission prompts and withdrawable any time in iOS Settings.

Your Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, and similar laws you have rights over your personal data. Since Echoelmusic collects no personal data, these are inherently protected:

  • Access: all data stays on your device, accessible to you at all times
  • Rectification: you can change all local data directly in the app
  • Erasure: delete the app to remove all data instantly
  • Portability: export your takes as WAV, and the immersive visual as MP4, at any time
  • Object / Restrict: not applicable, as we do not process your personal data
  • Withdraw consent: revoke permissions (HealthKit, camera, microphone) in iOS Settings at any time

California Privacy (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months. There is nothing to opt out of, because no personal information ever leaves your device. California residents' rights to know, delete, correct, and to non-discrimination are all satisfied by our collecting nothing.

International Users

Echoelmusic is available worldwide. Because all processing happens locally on your device and no data is transmitted to us or any third party, there are no international data transfers to safeguard. This policy is written in American English.

Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be communicated through the App Store update notes.
