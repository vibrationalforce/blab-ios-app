Echoel is a bio-reactive performance instrument. Your heart rate, heart-rate variability and breath drive sound, image and light in real time — the body becomes the controller.

Built for performance, installation, content and studio work — on iPhone.


THE BODY AS CONTROLLER

Connect Apple Watch, a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or use the iPhone camera (rPPG) to read your pulse:

- Heart rate shapes tempo and vibrato
- HRV and coherence shape brightness, harmonicity, texture and space
- Breath shapes the amplitude envelope and space

Not every source measures everything: the camera reads pulse, HRV and breath; a chest strap reads pulse and HRV; Apple Watch provides heart rate through Health. What isn't measured simply doesn't modulate.

No two takes are the same.


CREATE

- Bio-reactive additive synthesis engine (DDSP) — vDSP-accelerated, no external dependencies
- Design and save your own patches
- Composition: pick a genre and a key; your body generates in-key material you shape with the sound and character controls
- Concert pitch (A4 / Kammerton) set exactly to the cent; lock a BPM for tight loops or stay sync-free
- Effects: reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, harmonizer and more
- Immersive visual: the notes you play become colour in real time (perceptual pitch-to-hue), reacting to the music and your body
- Retroactive capture: keep the loop you just played
- Export the loop you just made as audio (WAV) or as MIDI (.mid) for your DAW — the MIDI file carries tempo, 4/4 and the key signature, and spans whole bars


CONNECT

- MIDI note input and output (output enabled in the routing panel)
- OSC output for live rigs and visuals
- ADM-OSC immersive object output
- Art-Net native light output for installations and stage


PRIVACY

Your biometrics stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and the app talks to no server of ours.

Four things send bio values outward, each only after you switch it on, and each only on your own local network: OSC output, ADM-OSC object output, Art-Net or sACN light output (heart rate, HRV, breath and coherence become DMX values), and sharing a live session with a nearby device. You can additionally opt in to writing heart rate and respiratory rate into Apple Health. What you create is yours.


ACCESSIBILITY

Designed accessibility-first: VoiceOver, large legible bio readouts, exact numeric entry for every parameter, and a 3 Hz visual flash cap for photosensitive safety.
